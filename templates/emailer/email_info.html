Configuration

Configure email settings in settings.py or via environment variables:

EMAIL_HOST_USER = 'your-email@gmail.com'
EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD = 'your-app-password'
Email Logs

All sent emails are logged in the system for tracking purposes. You can view the email log history from the admin panel or the email logs page.

Setup Instructions
  1. Enable 2-factor authentication on your Gmail account
  2. Generate an App Password at Google Account Settings
  3. Set the EMAIL_HOST_USER and EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD
  4. Test the email configuration
