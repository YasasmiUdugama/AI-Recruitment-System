CV Ranking Engine

TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity based ranking

Algorithm Overview

The ranking engine uses two powerful NLP techniques to match CVs against job descriptions:

TF-IDF Vectorization

Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency converts text into numerical vectors, weighing important keywords higher.

Cosine Similarity

Measures the cosine of the angle between two vectors, producing a similarity score from 0 to 1.

Features
  • Stop word removal for cleaner analysis
  • Unigram and bigram support for context
  • Automatic text preprocessing and cleaning
  • Top keyword extraction for each CV
  • Batch processing for large candidate pools
Score Interpretation

0.0 - 0.2: Low match

0.2 - 0.5: Moderate match

0.5 - 0.8: Good match

0.8 - 1.0: Excellent match
